The ePrivacy rules require consent before any storage or reading on a device that is not strictly necessary for a service the person asked for; GDPR sets the standard of that consent. ## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies we use on [website or application], what they do, and how you can control them. "Similar technologies" means anything that stores or reads information on your device: local storage, pixels and web beacons, software development kits in our mobile applications, and scripts that read device characteristics.

| Category | What it does | Consent |

## 4. How we ask for your consent

When you first visit, a banner asks you to choose. Nothing in the Functional, Analytics, Advertising or Social media categories is set until you accept it. The banner offers "Accept all", "Reject all" and "Choose" with equal prominence, and no category is pre-ticked. Closing the banner without choosing, scrolling or continuing to browse is not consent, and only strictly necessary technologies run.

Your choice is recorded, with the date, the version of this notice and the categories you accepted, in a strictly necessary cookie and in our consent record, so that we can show what you agreed to. *Record the consent in PIMS-REG-04 or the consent platform's log, keep the log for the period in PIMS-REG-07, and keep the banner wording of each version under GOV-REG-01.*

We ask again when we add a new category or a new third party, or after [Number] months, whichever comes first.

## 5. How to withdraw or change your choice

Open [the "Cookie settings" link in the footer] at any time, change the categories and save. Withdrawing consent stops the technologies in that category from the moment you save; data already collected is handled as described in the privacy notice. Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent: the same number of clicks, no account needed.

You can also delete cookies and block storage in your browser or device settings. If you do, our consent record is deleted too and the banner will appear again.
