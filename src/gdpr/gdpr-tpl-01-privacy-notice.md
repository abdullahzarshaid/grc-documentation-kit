*Guidance notes are in italics and are removed before publication. Every section below corresponds to an element the law requires; do not delete a section, write "not applicable" with a reason instead. Write for the reader, not the regulator: short sentences, no legal citations in the body, a reading age of about twelve. Each fact in this notice must match a row in PIMS-REG-01; the Privacy Officer checks the match before publication and after every change, and records the version in GOV-REG-01. Where data is obtained from someone other than the person, the notice is provided within one month, or at first contact or first disclosure if earlier.*

## 1. Who we are

[Organisation], [registered address], is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. *If a representative is appointed under GDPR Article 27, name it here with contact details. If the organisation acts as a processor for business customers, say so and point readers to the customer who decides how their data is used.*

You can contact us about anything in this notice at [contact channel]. *Where a Data Protection Officer is designated, give the DPO's contact details here; they may be a role mailbox rather than a name.*

## 2. What this notice covers

This notice explains what we do with personal data when you visit [website or application], create an account, use [Product or service], contact us, or receive marketing from us. *List every audience the notice covers. Employees and applicants have their own notices built from GDPR-TPL-02 and GDPR-TPL-03. Cookies and similar technologies are covered in the notice built from GDPR-TPL-04 and may be linked from here.*

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Providing [Product or service], including creating your account and delivering the features you use | Account details, content, usage information | Performance of our contract with you |

## 5. Automated decisions

*Required where decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made solely by automated means. If none, keep the first sentence.*

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. [If this changes, we will tell you how the decision is made, why it matters and how to ask for a person to review it.]

## 6. Who we share your data with

*List categories of recipients, and name them where the reader would reasonably expect it. Every processor listed here is in PIMS-REG-06. Sharing between group companies counts as sharing.*

- Service providers that act on our instructions: [hosting, email delivery, payment processing, customer support tools, analytics]. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
- [Group companies], for [purpose].
- Professional advisers, insurers and auditors, where needed.
- Authorities, regulators and courts, where the law requires or allows it.
- A buyer or successor, if our business or part of it is sold or reorganised, under confidentiality terms.

We do not sell your personal data.

Your data is stored in [country or region]. *State every country where data is stored, supported from or processed, including by processors; this must match PIMS-REG-05 and GDPR-REG-02.*

Where we transfer your data outside [region], we rely on [an adequacy decision by the relevant authority, standard contractual clauses approved by the relevant authority, binding corporate rules, or a derogation such as your explicit consent or the performance of your contract]. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use at the contact in section 1. *Name the mechanism per destination; never write "appropriate safeguards" alone.*

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to ask us for access to your personal data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting what was done before. You may object to direct marketing at any time and we will stop.

To exercise a right, contact us at [contact channel]. We will respond within one month and tell you if we need longer. We may ask you to confirm your identity. *The response process is PIMS-PRO-03; the letters are in GDPR-TPL-05.*

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Ours is [name of supervisory authority]. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, but you may contact the authority at any time.

## 10. Whether you must provide your data

*Required where data is collected from the person. State what is needed for the contract or by law and what happens if it is not provided.*

To open an account and use [Product or service] you must give us [the data marked as required on the sign-up form]. Without it we cannot provide the service. All other data is optional, and we tell you when it is.

## 11. Children

[Product or service] is not intended for children under [Number]. We do not knowingly collect data from them. *Where the service is offered to children, describe the age check and how parental consent is obtained, under PIMS-PRO-05.*

## 12. Changes to this notice

We will post any change here, with the date, and will tell you by [channel] before a change that affects how we use data we already hold about you. *Material changes follow PIMS-PRO-05 and are recorded in GOV-REG-01 with the previous versions kept.*

Version [Number], effective [Date].
