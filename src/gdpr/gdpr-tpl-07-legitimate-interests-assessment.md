*Guidance notes are in italics. One assessment per processing activity, completed by the activity owner with the Privacy Officer, approved before the processing starts, and entered in GDPR-REG-03. Legitimate interests is not available for processing by public authorities in the performance of their tasks, and is never a substitute for a condition under Article 9 where special category data is involved. Write for a reader who was not in the room: an auditor or a supervisory authority will read this if the processing is challenged.*

## 2. Purpose test: is there a legitimate interest?

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What interest is being pursued? | *State it specifically. "Business interests" fails; "detecting accounts created to abuse the free tier" passes.* |
| Whose interest is it? | [The organisation, a third party (name the category), both] |
| Is the interest lawful, clearly articulated and real rather than speculative? | [Yes or No], with reasons |
| What benefit does the processing bring, and to whom? | *The organisation, the people whose data is used, third parties, the public.* |
| What would happen if the processing did not take place? | *The loss or harm avoided by the processing.* |
| Is the processing something the people concerned would reasonably expect, given the relationship and the notice they received? | [Yes, Partly, No], with reasons |

**Purpose test outcome:** [Passed, Failed]. *A failed purpose test ends the assessment; find another basis or do not process.*

## 3. Necessity test: is the processing necessary for that interest?

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the processing actually help achieve the interest? | [Yes or No], with the reasoning |
| Could the interest be achieved with less data, less intrusive data, or a shorter retention? | *List the alternatives considered and why each was rejected. "No alternative considered" fails the test.* |
| Could it be achieved without processing personal data at all, for example with aggregated or anonymised data? | [Yes or No], with reasons |
| Is the data limited to what the purpose needs, and is the retention period the shortest that works? | [Yes or No], with the fields and the period |

**Necessity test outcome:** [Passed, Failed].

## 4. Balancing test: do the interests and rights of the people override the interest?

*Consider the nature of the data, the reasonable expectations of the people, the likely impact on them, and the safeguards that reduce it. Be honest about impact; an assessment that finds no impact at all is rarely credible.*

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Nature of the data | *Ordinary business contact data, behavioural data, financial data, data about children, location data, data that reveals something sensitive without being special category.* |
| Relationship with the people | *Existing customer, former customer, prospect never contacted, employee (note the imbalance of power), member of the public.* |
| Reasonable expectations | *What the notice told them, what the service implies, what practice in the sector is. Processing that would surprise a reasonable person weighs heavily against.* |
| Possible impact on the people | *Loss of control, nuisance, financial loss, discrimination, distress, exposure, chilling effect. Rate it Low, Medium or High and say why.* |
| Likelihood of that impact | [Low, Medium, High], with reasons |
| Vulnerable people | *Children, employees, people in financial difficulty, people with health conditions. State whether any are included and the effect on the balance.* |
| Safeguards applied | *Pseudonymisation, aggregation, access restriction, short retention, no use for decisions about the person, no sharing, a clear opt-out, human review. Each safeguard must be real and implemented, not planned.* |
| Opt-out | *Is an objection mechanism offered? For direct marketing it is mandatory and unconditional. Describe it and where it is presented.* |
| Transparency | *Where the people are told about this processing and the interest, with the notice reference.* |

**Balancing test outcome:** [The interest prevails, The interest prevails with the safeguards above, The rights of the people prevail].
