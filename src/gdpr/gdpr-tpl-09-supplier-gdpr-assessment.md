*Guidance notes are in italics. Send to every prospective supplier that will store, access or transmit personal data on the organisation's behalf, and to existing suppliers at their annual review. The security questions in GOV-TPL-04 are not repeated here; send both. Each answer needs evidence: a document, a screenshot, a contract clause or a certificate reference. An answer without evidence is scored as "not demonstrated". The Privacy Officer scores the outcome in section 8 and records it in GOV-REG-07 and, where the supplier is engaged, in PIMS-REG-06.*

## 2. Personal data facts

| # | Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Which categories of our personal data will you process, and for which purposes? | | |
| 2.2 | Will you use any of our personal data for your own purposes, including analytics, product improvement or training models? If yes, on what basis and can it be switched off? | | |
| 2.3 | Will you process any special category data or data about children? What additional controls apply? | | |

## 3. Security of personal data

| # | Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

| 3.2 | How is access to our data by your staff restricted, logged and reviewed? Can we see the logs on request? | | |

## 8. Outcome

*Completed by the Privacy Officer. A "Block" on any item means the supplier cannot be engaged until it is resolved; the blocks are: use of our data for the supplier's own purposes without a switch-off (2.2); no deletion at exit (2.6); no breach notification commitment within 48 hours (6.2); undisclosed or unlimited sub-processing (4.1, 4.2); a transfer with no mechanism (5.4); refusal to sign processor terms (7.1).*
