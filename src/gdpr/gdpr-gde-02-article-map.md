## 1. How to read this

Each row names an article, the obligation in plain words, and the kit documents or registers that address it. This is a map, not the law: the authoritative wording is in the regulation, and the map covers the articles a controller or processor operates against, not the whole text.

## 2. Principles and lawfulness

| Article | Obligation in plain words | Kit documents |
|---|---|---|
| Article 5 | Process data lawfully, fairly, transparently, for a purpose, minimally, accurately, no longer than needed, securely, and show it. | GDPR-POL-01; PIMS-REG-01; PIMS-REG-07 |
| Article 6 | Have a recorded lawful basis for every activity. | PIMS-REG-01; GDPR-TPL-07 |
| Article 7 | Make consent freely given, recorded and easy to withdraw. | PIMS-PRO-05; PIMS-REG-04 |
| Article 8 | Handle children's data and parental consent where services reach children. | PIMS-PRO-05 |
| Article 9 | Process special category data only under a recorded condition. | PIMS-REG-01 |
| Article 10 | Process criminal offence data only where law allows. | PIMS-REG-01 |
| Article 11 | Do not collect more data just to identify a person. | PIMS-PRO-03 |
