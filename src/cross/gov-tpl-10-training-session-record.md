## 1. How to use this record

*The trainer completes sections 2 to 6 for every live or facilitated awareness session and returns the record to the Information Security Manager within five working days. Attendees sign section 4 in person or confirm electronically. The Information Security Manager enters each attendee's completion and result in GOV-REG-09 and files this record with the annual training plan. For online modules the learning platform's completion report replaces this form.*
