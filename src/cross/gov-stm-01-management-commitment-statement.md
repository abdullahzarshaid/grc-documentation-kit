## 1. Statement

The leadership of [Organisation] regards the protection of information, and of the people whose personal data we process, as a condition of doing business, not an addition to it. We have established an information security and privacy management system covering the scope in ISMS-STM-01, and we hold ourselves accountable for its results.

## 2. What leadership commits to

1. **Direction.** We have approved GOV-POL-03 and the objectives in ISMS-PLN-01, and we will keep both consistent with the strategy and risk appetite of the organisation.
2. **Resources.** We will fund and staff the management system each financial year, including the people, training, tools and external support needed to treat the risks recorded in GOV-REG-05 within the agreed dates.
3. **Integration.** Security and privacy requirements form part of how we plan projects, select suppliers, develop software, hire people and run operations. They are not handled by a separate team after the fact.
4. **Roles.** We have appointed an Information Security Manager and a Privacy Officer, given them the authority described in GOV-POL-01, and we expect every manager to carry the responsibilities assigned to them.
5. **Review.** We will attend management review under GOV-PRO-03 at least once a year, consider every mandatory input, and record our decisions and the resources we assign.
6. **Improvement.** We will act on audit results, incidents, measurement results and nonconformities through GOV-PRO-04, and we will not accept a risk above appetite without recording who accepted it and why.
7. **Compliance.** We will identify and meet our legal, regulatory and contractual obligations as recorded in GOV-REG-04.
8. **Culture.** We will lead by example, complete the same training as everyone else, and support anyone who reports a weakness or an incident in good faith.

## 3. How this commitment is demonstrated

Auditors and interested parties may test this statement against the approved budget, steering committee minutes, management review minutes (GOV-TPL-03), the action tracker (GOV-REG-22) and the objectives tracker (GOV-REG-21).
