## 1. Purpose

This policy tells everyone who uses [Organisation] information, devices, accounts and services what they may and may not do with them, so that ordinary daily work does not become the way an attacker gets in or information gets out.

## 2. Scope

It applies to employees, contractors, temporary staff, interns and supplier personnel who hold an [Organisation] account or device, at any location, and to personal devices used for work under statement 4.9.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All users | Follow this policy, acknowledge it, report incidents and breaches. |
| Information Security Manager | Owns the policy, keeps the approved services list, investigates suspected breaches with Human Resources. |
| IT Manager | Configures devices and services so that the policy can be enforced and evidenced. |
| Human Resources | Records acknowledgements in GOV-REG-23 and runs the disciplinary process. |

## 4. Policy statements

4.1 **Permitted use.** [Organisation] information, devices, accounts and services are provided for its business. Limited personal use of internet and email is permitted where it does not interfere with work, break a law or this policy, or expose [Organisation] information.

4.2 **Devices.** Users work on devices issued and managed by [Organisation], keep the GOV-PRO-22 controls switched on, install no software outside the approved catalogue without the IT Manager's approval, and hold no administrative rights on their own device.

4.3 **Locking and loss.** Users lock their screen whenever they step away and report a lost or stolen device within one hour under GOV-PRO-07.

4.4 **Accounts.** Each user uses only accounts issued in their own name, never shares a password, one-time code or signed-in session with anyone, including IT staff, and follows GOV-POL-06.

4.5 **Email and messaging.** Users send Confidential and Restricted information only through the channels permitted in GOV-PRO-16, never forward work email or information to a personal account, and report suspected phishing through the report button rather than replying or clicking.

4.6 **Internet.** Users do not access or distribute illegal, discriminatory, harassing or sexually explicit material, bypass web filtering or security monitoring, or download software or media from untrusted sources.

4.7 **Cloud services.** Users store and process [Organisation] information only in services on the approved list kept by the Information Security Manager; a new service is assessed under GOV-PRO-06 before use. Personal cloud storage, note-taking and messaging services are not used for work information above Public.

4.8 **Removable media.** Removable media is used only with the Information Security Manager's written approval of the specific need; approved media is encrypted, issued by the IT Manager, recorded in GOV-REG-24 and disposed of under GOV-PRO-16.

4.9 **Personal devices.** A personal phone or computer is used for work only when enrolled in [Organisation]'s device management under GOV-PRO-22, with a passcode, encryption, current updates, [Organisation]'s ability to remove its data, and no Confidential or Restricted information outside the managed applications. Nobody else uses the device while work data is on it.

4.10 **AI tools.** Users use AI tools only when approved in AI-REG-04, for the uses listed there, under AI-POL-01. Personal data, customer confidential material, credentials, keys and source code are never entered into such a tool unless the register entry permits it. The user checks any output before relying on it and remains responsible for it.

4.11 **Information handling.** Users classify, label, store, share, print and dispose of information under GOV-PRO-16, keep desks and screens clear under GOV-POL-04, and work away from the office under GOV-POL-05.

4.12 **Intellectual property and testing.** Users do not copy licensed software or infringe copyright, do not take [Organisation] information, source code or customer data with them when they leave, and do not probe or attempt to defeat any system's security controls unless authorised in writing.

4.13 **Monitoring notice.** [Organisation] logs the use of its devices, networks, accounts and services and may inspect that activity to protect security and investigate a suspected breach of policy or law. Monitoring is proportionate, follows GOV-PRO-14 and is described in GDPR-TPL-02. Users have no expectation of privacy on [Organisation] systems beyond what the law requires.

4.14 **Reporting.** Users report a suspected incident, mistaken disclosure or policy breach immediately under GOV-PRO-07. Prompt reporting of a mistake is expected; concealing one is itself a breach.

4.15 **Consequences.** A breach is handled under the disciplinary process in GOV-PRO-18 and may lead to withdrawal of access, dismissal, contract termination or referral to law enforcement.

4.16 **Acknowledgement.** Each user acknowledges this policy at induction, after each material change and yearly thereafter, recorded by Human Resources in GOV-REG-23. Access to Confidential or Restricted information depends on a current acknowledgement.

## 5. Records and evidence

- GOV-REG-23 Policy acknowledgement register.
- The approved services list and AI-REG-04 AI tool approval register.
- Incident reports in GOV-REG-08 and disciplinary records held by Human Resources.

## 6. Exceptions

Only the Information Security Manager may grant an exception, in writing, for a named person, a stated reason and a fixed period of at most twelve months, recorded under GOV-PRO-24.

## 7. Review and maintenance

The Information Security Manager reviews this policy annually, after an incident caused by misuse, and when a new class of service or tool comes into use. Changes follow GOV-PRO-01 and trigger re-acknowledgement under 4.16.
